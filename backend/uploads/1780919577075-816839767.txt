Application Use Case
MediCare is a comprehensive digital health portal designed to facilitate end-to-end patient-to-doctor consultations, scheduling, and medical education. It serves as a unified virtual clinic where patients can explore verified medical professionals, schedule virtual or physical visits, chat securely, and learn about preventive healthcare through articles. or with the help of ai assistance 

Problem statement 
Navigating healthcare services is traditionally disjointed. Patients face several hurdles:
* Long waiting times on phone calls to schedule appointments.
* Difficulty verifying doctor specializations, bios, and real availability.
* Lack of secure, encrypted channels for pre-consultation chat, voice, and video messaging.
* Fragmented sources of reliable, medical-category educational material.

MediCare solves this by bridging the gap between patients and doctors, offering streamlined scheduling, robust search filters, HIPAA-compliant chat and calling mechanisms, and structured medical articles. and ai assistance
